The first version rebuilt the top-level structure every frame, relative to the camera, and cost about
2 ms of CPU time a frame: 187 fps against 236 at view distance 16, and 132 against 185 at 32. Placing
sections around an anchor that seldom moves, each in a slot of its own, means the top level is rebuilt
only when sections come, go or change.

Stress run at view distance 12 (scratch script, not kept): breaking a block rebuilt its section in
place; teleports of 5,000 and 8,000 blocks, which move the anchor, streamed every section out and in
again with nothing left waiting; turning the option off and on in the pause menu let everything go
and built it all again. No wgpu validation errors.
