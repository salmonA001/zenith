Repeat runs of the same build on the busy machine gave 387-432 fps at view distance 8, 239-282 at
16 and 153-177 at 32, while the CPU time a frame stayed within 0.2 ms. The time to a complete view
varied by under half a second. Sampling one run at view distance 32 every few seconds showed frames
of up to 30 ms in the first ten seconds and none over 9 ms after that.
